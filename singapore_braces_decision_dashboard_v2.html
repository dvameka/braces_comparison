Consultation checklist

Ask every clinic the same questions so that you compare like-for-like:

  1. Who personally manages my case? Ask for the dentist/orthodontist's name and whether they are on the Singapore Dental Council Dental Specialist Register for Orthodontics.
  2. What is my total metal price and ceramic price including GST?
  3. Are all routine adjustment visits included until treatment is complete? If not, what happens if treatment runs 6–12 months longer?
  4. Are OPG + lateral ceph X-rays, photos, scans/models and treatment planning included?
  5. Are bracket removal/debonding and one upper + lower retainer set included? Ask which retainer type.
  6. What is excluded? Extractions, fillings, scaling/polishing, emergency repairs, broken brackets, sterilisation/infection-control fees and replacement retainers.
  7. How often are reviews expected and what treatment duration is estimated for my specific bite?
  8. If I relocate or need to transfer care, what records/transfer fees apply?
